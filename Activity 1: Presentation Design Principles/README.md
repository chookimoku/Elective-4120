Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

Activity Description

This activity involved creating a promotional graphic or publicity material (pubmat) for a Free Coffee Booth organized by the Ateneo de Davao University School of Nursing. The poster was designed to advertise the booth and encourage students and other members of the university community to visit.

The task required the use of visual elements such as colors, typography, illustrations, logos, and layout to create an attractive and informative promotional material. It also emphasized the importance of presenting event details clearly while making the design visually appealing.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how to apply basic graphic design principles when creating promotional materials. I realized that choosing appropriate colors, fonts, and visual elements helps make a design more attractive and easier to understand.

I also learned the importance of organizing information through a clear layout so that viewers can quickly identify the event, location, date, and other important details. One challenge was balancing the visual elements while keeping the poster informative and readable. I addressed this by organizing the content and making the main announcement more prominent.

In the future, I can apply these skills when creating digital advertisements, event announcements, and other promotional materials for academic activities and organizations.
