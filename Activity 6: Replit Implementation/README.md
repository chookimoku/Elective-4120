Activity 6: Replit Implementation

Activity Description

This activity focused on using Replit AI to develop a one-page website based on a business idea. I chose to create a pet shop called “Paws & Whiskers Pet Shop,” which offers pet food, toys, accessories, and grooming supplies.

The website was designed to introduce the business and present its products and services through sections such as Home, About Us, Products, Special Offers, Why Choose Us, Testimonials, and Contact Us. This activity demonstrated how AI tools can assist in creating a website even with limited coding experience.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how to use Replit AI to develop a basic website using prompts and instructions. I also gained a better understanding of website structure, content organization, and how digital platforms can help promote a business.

One challenge I encountered was providing clear instructions to the AI so that the website would match my business concept. I addressed this by specifying the business name, purpose, and sections I wanted to include.

In the future, I can apply these skills when creating websites for personal projects, small businesses, and other digital initiatives. This activity also helped me appreciate how AI can support creativity, problem-solving, and digital entrepreneurship.
