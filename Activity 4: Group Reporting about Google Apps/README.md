Activity 4: Group Reporting about Google Apps

Activity Description

This activity involved conducting a group report about Google applications, specifically Google Forms and Google Meet. The purpose was to explore how these digital tools work, their features, and how they can be used for communication, collaboration, and information gathering.

As part of the activity, our group prepared a PowerPoint presentation discussing Google Forms and Google Meet. The presentation introduced their functions and demonstrated how these applications can be useful in academic and professional settings.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned more about the features and practical uses of Google Forms and Google Meet. I understood how Google Forms can be used to create surveys, quizzes, and collect information, while Google Meet provides a platform for online meetings, discussions, and collaboration.

One challenge of group reporting was organizing the information and presenting the applications in a clear and understandable way. Working with my group helped us divide the tasks, share ideas, and combine our contributions into one presentation.

In the future, I can apply what I learned by using these applications for academic activities, online discussions, surveys, and professional communication. These tools can also be useful in healthcare settings for coordination, information gathering, and virtual meetings.
