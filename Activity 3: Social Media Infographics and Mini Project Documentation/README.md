Activity 3: One-Page Infographic and Project Documentation Report

Activity Description

This activity focused on identifying a real-world problem and presenting a possible technology-based solution through visual and written outputs. My chosen topic was water scarcity, presented through an infographic titled “Our Future Is Drying Up!”

The first output was a one-page infographic that highlighted the causes, statistics, effects, and possible solutions to water scarcity using illustrations, icons, and short explanations. The second output was a project documentation report proposing a Smart Water Conservation and Monitoring System, a technology-based concept designed to help households and communities monitor water consumption, detect leaks, and promote responsible water usage.

Together, these outputs aimed to raise awareness about water scarcity and demonstrate how technology can contribute to addressing environmental challenges.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how to research a real-world issue, organize relevant information, and communicate important ideas through visual and written presentations. I also gained a better understanding of how technology can be used to propose practical solutions to environmental problems.

One challenge I encountered was presenting a broad topic like water scarcity in a concise and understandable way while keeping the infographic visually engaging. I addressed this by organizing the information into sections covering its causes, impacts, and possible solutions.

This activity helped me appreciate the role of information technology in addressing real-world challenges. In the future, I can apply these skills when creating awareness campaigns, presenting research, and developing technology-based project proposals.
