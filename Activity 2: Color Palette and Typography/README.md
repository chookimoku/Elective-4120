Activity 2: Personal Branding Kit

Activity Description

This activity focused on creating a personal branding kit that represents my identity, personality, values, and professional aspirations. I developed my personal brand under the name “MAI,” creating a consistent visual identity that reflects both my personal style and my future career in nursing.

The branding kit consisted of four components: a personal logo, a tagline, a color palette and typography set, and a customized resume header. I used sage green, mauve pink, blush pink, warm ivory, and warm taupe to establish a feminine, elegant, and professional aesthetic. I also selected different typography styles to maintain consistency throughout the designs.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how to develop a personal brand using visual design elements such as logos, colors, typography, and taglines. I realized that personal branding is not only about creating an attractive design but also about communicating one’s identity and professional goals.

One challenge I encountered was choosing colors and fonts that would complement each other while maintaining a professional appearance. I addressed this by developing a coordinated color palette and selecting typography styles that worked well together.

This activity helped me understand the importance of having a consistent professional identity. In the future, I can apply these skills when designing my resume, creating professional documents, and presenting myself as a future healthcare professional.
