Activity 5: Exploring and Improving AI Prompts

Activity Description

This activity focused on exploring how artificial intelligence responds to different prompts, particularly in AI image generation. Using Gemini, I created an image based on an original prompt and then improved the prompt by adding more specific details.

For the original prompt, I asked Gemini to create a cute golden retriever playing at the beach. I then improved the prompt by describing the dog’s appearance, its joyful expression, the pink ball, and the sunny beach setting. This allowed me to compare how the quality and details of an AI-generated image can change depending on the instructions provided.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned that writing clear and detailed prompts helps AI generate outputs that are closer to what I imagine. I also learned how to improve prompts by adding specific descriptions and instructions.

One challenge I encountered was that my original prompt was too general, which gave the AI more freedom in interpreting the request. I addressed this by adding details about the subject, appearance, objects, and background.

In the future, I can apply these skills when using AI tools for creative projects, visual presentations, and other academic tasks. This activity also taught me the importance of reviewing and improving AI-generated outputs rather than simply accepting the first result.
